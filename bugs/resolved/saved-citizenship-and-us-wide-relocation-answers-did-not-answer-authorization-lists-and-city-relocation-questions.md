# Saved citizenship and U.S.-wide relocation answers did not answer authorization lists and city relocation questions

The candidate asked on 2026-11-27: "Yes I would relocate anywhere in the US. I am a citizen. I feel like these questions were already answered, find out why they are not beign automstically answered".
Held runs whose relocation questions waited for the candidate (blocker be855a8fa67): Veeva Systems <run id> Q1 and <run id> Q1, Base Power Company <run id> Q2, Trimble <run id> Q7, Strava <run id> Q6, Reliable Robotics <run id> Q3 and Q4, and Nasdaq <run id> Q3.
Held runs whose work-authorization lists waited for the candidate: Granite Dynamics <run id> Q3, Trimble <run id> Q1, and Vital Lyfe <run id> Q3.
Cause 1: a relocation question naming a bare city ("… willing to relocate to Boston?") mapped to that city's own scope, which only an answer saved for that exact city answers, while the candidate's answer is saved for the United States.
Cause 2: the destination kept what followed the city ("san francisco for the summer internship (june-august)") and read a purpose as a place ("meet this requirement"), and the stop re-check fed the stored scope back as the question's location, so the wrong scope persisted.
Cause 3: Trimble Q7 offers two options that start with Yes ("Yes: I live nearby or the role is remote", "Yes: I am willing to relocate"), and the saved relocation Yes picked neither.
Cause 4: no rule let the saved U.S. citizenship pick a sentence option of a work-authorization list, and Granite's wording "legally eligible for employment in the United States" was not mapped to work authorization at all.
Fixed in Track 62: a saved U.S.-wide relocation Yes answers a relocation question naming a U.S. city, one way only (`packages/agent/src/candidate-service.ts`, `placeInUnitedStates` in `packages/agent/src/question-mapper.ts`, `postingPlaces` from `postingQuestionContext` in `packages/agent/src/saved-answers.ts`, `packages/domain/src/question-memory.ts`).
Destinations are read without their tail, a purpose or an unnamed site is not a place, and a relocation question that also asks about on-site work, living nearby, dates, a temporary or permanent move, who pays, or remote work is broader and answered only when each part is settled (`relocationPlace` in `question-mapper.ts`, `relocationQuestionParts` in `packages/domain/src/fact-options.ts`).
A saved relocation Yes picks the one Yes option naming relocating among several (`savedYesNoOption`), and a saved U.S. citizenship Yes picks the one work-authorization option citizenship makes true (`citizenAuthorizationOption` in `fact-options.ts`, `usCitizenSettles` in `saved-answers.ts`, `resolveFields` in `packages/agent/src/browser-orchestrator.ts`, `packages/browser/src/autofill-planner.ts`); "eligible for employment" now maps to work authorization (`question-mapper.ts`).
The rules are described in `docs/technical/10-candidate-memory-policy/012-us-citizenship-and-us-wide-relocation-2026-11-27.md`.
