# A question naming a university was not compared with the profile school

CodeRabbit's "Are you an undergrad, masters or PhD student from <a named university>?" (an Ashby Yes/No radio) waited for the candidate on 5 runs (4 cancelled, 1 still held), although the profile's school is a different institution.
Cause: the question is unmapped, no planner rule compared a school a question names with the profile's school, and the AI pause review never sees the profile school by design.
A saved enrollment answer could also have answered "Are you a current student at <another school>?" Yes, because the mapper read any current-student or full-time-student wording as `enrolled_full_time`.
Fixed in Track 57: `namedSchoolRelation` (`packages/domain/src/application-history.ts`) tells whether a named school is the profile's school ("same"), clearly another institution ("different": both full institution names, no abbreviation, no shared word besides university, college, or institute), or "ambiguous"; the planner rule `namedSchoolDecision` (`packages/browser/src/autofill-planner.ts`) answers a present-tense Yes/No question whether the candidate studies at one named school No for another institution, Yes for the profile's own school only while the graduation date is ahead with no full-time or part-time qualifier and an undergraduate level among any levels named, and leaves anything else (abbreviations, a campus or its system, two schools, past or future wording, high school) paused; the mapper no longer maps a student question naming one school to `enrolled_full_time` (`packages/agent/src/question-mapper.ts`; docs/technical/06-job-posting-model/310 and 10-candidate-memory-policy/065).
Regression: the Track 57 cases in `pnpm test:browser-worker:fast` (the planner rule), `pnpm test:domain` (the comparison), and `pnpm test:agent` (the mapper exclusion).
