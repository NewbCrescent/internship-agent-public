On 2026-11-28 the candidate gave answers about clearance eligibility, the ACT, full-time availability for the saved summer window, and the county of the current address (values not recorded here).
The clearance answers were already saved, yet clearance questions still paused; the ACT, full-time availability, and county questions had no fact to save the answer in.
Cause C1: a saved No to holding a clearance only tried a plain "No" option, so a clearance-level list ending in "None of the Above" (Lockheed Martin <run id> and <run id>, "Security Clearance - Active") paused with "saved answer not among the options".

Cause C3: "Are you eligible to obtain and maintain a U.S. Government Clearance (Requires U.S Citizenship)?" (Voyager Technologies <run id>) matched both the clearance and the citizenship groups and was held as combined, although the citizenship words are only a requirement note.

Cause K1: "County" text boxes (GDIT <run id>, Example Insurance <run id>, Corvane <run id> and <run id>) and a "<County>, <ST>" list (Idaho National Laboratory <run id>) had no fact.
