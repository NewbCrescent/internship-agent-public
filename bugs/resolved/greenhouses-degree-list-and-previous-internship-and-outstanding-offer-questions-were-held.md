# Greenhouse's Degree list and previous-internship and outstanding-offer questions were held

fixture: captured 2026-11-28 (the stored labels and options of the held runs, replayed read-only; the tests use fictional names and values)

On 2026-11-28 five held Greenhouse runs paused on their education "Degree" list (Appian <run id> and <run id>, Atoms <run id>, Aquatic Capital Management <run id>, Ironvale <run id>), and the AI pause review returned needs_candidate for each in pass 37.
Cause D1: Greenhouse's Degree list offers both "Bachelors" and "Bachelor's Degree"; for the profile's B.A., `chooseDegreeOption` found no option of the same kind and two general bachelor's options, and two options that fit equally asked.
The candidate also gave two answers on 2026-11-28 (values not recorded here) that had no fact to be saved in: how many internships were completed before, and whether there are outstanding offers or deadlines.
Held runs ask the second in several shapes: "Do you have any outstanding offers or deadlines?" (Yes/No lists and a text box), "Are you holding any outstanding offers?", "Do you currently have any offers from other firms or deadlines we should be aware of?", "Do you currently have an offer? If so, what is your deadline to make a decision by?", and "Do you have an active offer deadline or another recruiting-process deadline we should be aware of? If yes, please share the deadline date …".
Fixed in Track 87: spellings of the one general bachelor's answer (the same words ignoring case, apostrophes, a plural "s", and "Degree") count as one answer and the first offered is chosen, while options differing in any other word still ask, and a list naming no bachelor's takes its one plain "Undergraduate" option (`chooseDegreeOption`, `packages/domain/src/application-history.ts`); the `prior_internships_count` and `has_outstanding_offers` facts (`packages/domain/src/candidate-facts.ts`), their option rules (`internshipCountOption`, `priorInternshipThreshold`, `packages/domain/src/fact-options.ts`), mapper wordings (`packages/agent/src/question-mapper.ts`), and the at-least-one text-box rule in `factOptionDecision` (`packages/browser/src/autofill-planner.ts`).
The rules are described in `docs/technical/10-candidate-memory-policy/015-prior-internships-outstanding-offers-and-degree-spellings-2026-11-28.md`.
Read-only replay (2026-11-28, `questions-recheck.ts --profile real`, dry run) before: "open pauses: 782; would be answered by saved answers: 17 (…, profile_field 2)"; after: "open pauses: 802; would be answered by saved answers: 22 (…, profile_field 7)" (the held runs grew between the two replays); the new matches are the five Degree stops above.
The internship and offers questions are answered only after the candidate's answers are saved with `pnpm profile:facts`; no value is in a tracked file.
Regression: `pnpm test:domain` (`testing-harness/domain/track87-internships-offers-degree.test.ts`), `pnpm test:agent` (`testing-harness/agent/held-question-corpus-track87.test.ts`), and `pnpm test:memory` (`testing-harness/agent/memory-service.integration.test.ts`).
Held on purpose: a follow-up that only asks to name the offers or their deadlines, any question that also asks to name them when the saved answer is Yes, and internship counts qualified by a field, paid work, a company, or co-ops.
