Feature labels include `foundation`, `profile`, `remember`, `database`, `dashboard`, `github`, `evidence`, `jobs`, `ingestion`, `discovery`, `matching`, `writing`, `verification`, `applications`, `outcomes`, `browser`, `scoreboard`, and `agent-workflow`.
The `profile` gate covers separate real/demo SQLite files; a browser switch that checks candidate, job, evidence, and base-resume isolation plus resume-entry editing and a one-page PDF preview; resume-entry revisions, approval, verification, and freezing; Handshake-channel graduation scoping in mapping, resolution, and eligibility; base resume storage and checksum stops; generated-resume layout and PDF conversion; sign-in detection; and a real-Chromium fixture upload of a rendered PDF.
Agent-created Workday accounts are covered by `test:agent:workday-account` (fast: password policy, settings, verification-link acceptance), `test:agent:workday-account:local` (the owner-only accounts file, the hold gate, mailbox polling with a fake mailbox), `test:browser-worker:local` (pipeline account scenarios with an in-memory page), and `test:browser:workday-account` (real Chromium against routed synthetic Workday pages); the Gmail IMAP adapter itself is not exercised by any suite, since that needs the real mailbox.
The Gmail outcome watcher is covered by `test:agent:mail` (fast: rules, matching, DM text, service renderers) and `test:agent:mail:local` (a fake IDLE session against a disposable workspace); the Gmail IDLE adapter itself is not exercised by any suite either.
`test:browser:fixture` runs under tsx, whose keepNames output references an `__name` helper inside functions passed to `page.evaluate`; the fixture defines that helper in the page.
Production builds do not emit it.
The `github` gate covers deterministic extraction, disposable-SQLite incremental sync, and the seeded browser review flow.
The `jobs` gate covers normalization contracts, disposable-SQLite ingestion and revision, and the seeded browser ingestion flow.
The `discovery` gate covers batch parsing, bounded parsing of all five selected GitHub list formats and full-public-posting resolution, disposable-SQLite feedback and rate limits, and browser review controls.
The `outcomes` gate covers transition guards, disposable-SQLite funnel and cost aggregation, and browser employer updates.
The `matching` gate covers eligibility ordering contracts, disposable-SQLite match persistence with status transitions, and the seeded browser analysis flow.
