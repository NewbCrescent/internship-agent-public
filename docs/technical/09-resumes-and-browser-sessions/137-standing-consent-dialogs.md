**A consent dialog over the form (Track 40).**
On Lockheed Martin run <run id> (Eightfold, 2026-11-25) seven lists paused with "a dialog covers the form", and Track 39's capture showed what covered them: a `role="dialog"` `aria-modal="true"` "Privacy Notice and Security Agreement" with a Close (×) button, "Cancel", and "I Agree".
Its text is the company's system-use acknowledgment (use of the system is monitored; unauthorized use is prohibited), two closed sections ("How we use your resume/CV:", "Your rights:"), a link to the Job Applicant Privacy Notice, and one operative sentence: "By clicking I Agree, you acknowledge that you have read and understand this notice and the Job Applicant Privacy Notice and consent to the collection and processing of your personal information as described above and in our Job Applicant Privacy Notice."
That is a privacy-notice acknowledgment and data-processing consent that is a condition of applying, and its truth depends on no fact about the candidate.
The resume upload opens it, not the form's load: recognition opened and read every list in that run, which the dialog would have prevented; the run applied the upload first, then the text boxes (filled without a hit test), and the first list click after them was covered; the dialog has its own "How we use your resume/CV" section; and the capture's Resume/CV area shows no file yet.
The form's "I have read and understand and I agree to the above" list under "STATEMENT OF AGREEMENT" is an accuracy certification at the end of the form; it was never clicked in that run, did not open the dialog, and is not answered by it.
`packages/browser/src/standing-dialog.ts` reads each visible modal dialog outside the cookie-consent containers (`readStandingDialogsIn`, the same selector as Track 39's reader): its label, its full text with one line per block element and the bodies of closed `<details>` sections included, buttons, inputs, scripts, and frames skipped, and its visible buttons, tagged for the click.
`standingDialogDecision` then decides with the existing standing-consent classifier (`standingStatementDecision`, `classifyStandingStatement`, `textMessageTopic`, `affirmativeOption`, `packages/domain/src/standing-consent.ts`), never a looser one.
The dialog is left to the candidate unless all of these hold: exactly one dialog is open; it holds no form field and no frame; its text is not clipped (no line ending "…", no "Read more" or "Expand" control, no collapsed control with nothing behind it, no closed section with an empty body, no section heading with nothing under it, and under 6,000 characters); it has no sign-in, password, session, CAPTCHA, robot-check, or cookie wording.
Fact, marketing, and text-message wording anywhere in the dialog, closed sections included, keeps it with the candidate, even when the candidate saved the fact; a field asking the same fact is still answered only from saved facts.
It needs exactly one enabled agree button ("I Agree", "Agree", "Accept", "I accept", "Acknowledge", "I consent", optionally "… and continue"), every other button being Cancel, Close, ×, Decline, No, or Back, and a disabled agree button leaves it.
Every operative sentence ("By clicking…", "I agree…", "you consent to…") must be accepted by `standingStatementDecision` under the candidate's settings, and a sentence that acknowledges reading a notice also needs the attestation setting, so the Lockheed dialog needs both `INTERNSHIP_AGENT_ACCEPT_DATA_CONSENT=1` and `INTERNSHIP_AGENT_SIGN_ATTESTATIONS=1`.
Only the agree button is ever clicked, with a trusted click after checking its name; Close and Cancel never.
`applyDecisions` (`packages/browser/src/handshake-executor.ts`) tries it right after a successful upload (looking up to 1 s for a dialog) and when a field is left unfilled with "a dialog covers the form", and then tries that field once more; it clicks at most 3 agree buttons per fill and never while a CAPTCHA is shown.
The orchestrator passes the candidate's settings at the start of a run, on continue, and when the approved answers are applied again before the submit click (`coveringDialogCapture`, `packages/agent/src/browser-orchestrator.ts`).
Each click is logged on the timeline with the exact statement and the dialog's full text (`logStandingDialogAcceptance`, `packages/agent/src/standing-consent.ts`): "Accepted data consent and attestation: '<statement>' — clicked "I Agree" on the dialog "<label>" over the form. Dialog text: '<text>'.", plus "The dialog was still shown after the click." when it did not go away.
A covering dialog left with a setting on is logged once per fill with the reason (`logStandingDialogLeft`): "Left the dialog "<label>" over the form for you: <reason>.", for example "it includes a marketing, newsletter, or job-alert opt-in".
With both settings off nothing is logged and nothing changes.
Track 37's announcement close (`chooseAnnouncementClose`) and Track 39's capture are unchanged: every dialog left to the candidate still ends in "a dialog covers the form", with one capture for the run.
What the real dialog's closed sections say is not known until the next real Eightfold run; if they hold fact, marketing, or uncovered wording, or their bodies are not in the page until opened, the dialog is left and the "Left …" event names the rule.
Whether agreeing lets Eightfold keep the uploaded file is likewise confirmed only by the next real run.
