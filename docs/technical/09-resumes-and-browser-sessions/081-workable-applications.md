**Workable applications (Track 77).**
Workable hosts employers' application forms at `apply.workable.com/<account>/j/<shortcode>/apply/`, one page behind the posting's overview at `/j/<shortcode>/`; `applicationUrlFor` opens the `/apply/` page directly.
The form was read from a read-only, headful capture of Digital Control's application on 2026-11-27 (nothing clicked or typed) and Workable's public bundle (`careers.<hash>.js`), which supplied its texts.
Each Yes/No question is a `fieldset role="radiogroup"` labelled by its question span (`aria-labelledby`), and each option a `div role="radio" aria-checked` over a `label` holding a native radio with `aria-hidden="true"` and `tabindex="-1"` and a "YES" or "NO" span.
Those radios were dropped as react-select shadow inputs, so run <run id> filled nine fields and never saw the two required questions; a radio inside a `role="radio"` element is now read (`collectControlsIn`, `packages/browser/src/handshake-executor.ts`), as one radio question with the fieldset's question as its label and YES and NO as its options.
A chosen option is read back from its `aria-checked="true"` (or the radio being checked), and choosing clicks the `role="radio"` option itself and waits up to 2 s for it to show chosen, never the DOM-dispatch fallback; an option that does not keep the choice is reported unfilled.
Workable's hidden `city`, `postcode`, and `country` inputs (aria-hidden, tabindex -1, in 1px containers) stay unread, and its "Import resume from" autofill is a dropdown button, not an upload; the only upload is the Resume input.
`classifyAts` reads `apply.workable.com` as kind `workable` (its footer says "Powered byWorkable"), which is confirmed automatically only on Workable's own hosts (`atsKindOwnsUrl`) and is not in `ATS_KINDS_NEEDING_APPROVAL`.
Workable leaving the approval-only set widens what the auto-submit policy may submit; it was approved under the candidate's full authority of 2026-11-27, relayed by the manager, on the same basis as Dover: one page, one "Submit application" control, and a success text the confirmation pattern reads.
