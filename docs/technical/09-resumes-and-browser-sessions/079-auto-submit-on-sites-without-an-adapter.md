**Auto-submit on sites without an adapter (Track 80).**
On 2026-11-28 the candidate asked for no more submit approvals ("I SONT WANT DO TO APPEOVALS I JUST WANT A SUMMER INTERNSHOP WITH ME DOING AS LITTLE AS POSSIBLE"), so the auto-submit policy now submits clean runs on every site kind but SmartRecruiters.
`autoSubmitBlockers` no longer holds a run for its site type: a kind in `ATS_KINDS_NEEDING_APPROVAL`, an unrecognized site, and a several-page form walked step by step (Track 74) pass when nothing else holds them.
SmartRecruiters (`ATS_KINDS_NEVER_AUTO_SUBMITTED`, `packages/domain/src/ats.ts`) stays the candidate's to approve: its posting page sits in front of a separate application page the agent does not read, and its pages showed DataDome to the agent's browser.
`approveRunSubmissionAutomatically` takes an unconfirmed run's site type as the policy's own act, named in its approval event: the suggested kind when the run's address is on that kind's own site (`atsKindOwnsUrl`), otherwise unknown, so the generic filler's gates apply.
Membership in `ATS_KINDS_NEEDING_APPROVAL` still fills a kind's runs in the confirm band, so the candidate confirms the kind on runs the policy does not submit.
`uncapturedSiteRun` (`packages/domain/src/submission-mode.ts`) names a run on a site whose confirmation page the agent has not captured: a kind outside `ATS_KINDS_WITH_CAPTURED_CONFIRMATION` (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Handshake, Ashby, Dover), an unrecognized site, or a stepped run.
On such a site, and on any unconfirmed or stepped run, `autoSubmitBlockers` also holds a run whose decisions hold no application content (`hasApplicationContent`: an uploaded resume or document, or the candidate's name with the email), since Track 77 found unknown sites whose only captured field was a newsletter or sign-up email box; a submit control reading Subscribe, Sign up, Notify me, or Join is never the final submit.
Under the policy such a run's click goes only to a control whose text reads as the final submit ("Submit", "Submit application", "Apply"; never Next, Upload, a cookie button, or a sign-in; `finalSubmitHoldReason` in `packages/browser/src/submit-gate.ts`, reading the page's `submitControlLabel`), otherwise the run is held with the "Submit button" safeguard pause and nothing is clicked.
After the click, a page that reads as a confirmation, and an emailed-code step the agent cannot enter, are held as a possible submission (`SubmissionUnverified`, `packages/browser/src/submission-outcome.ts`): the run is failed with its approval kept and its post-submit evidence saved, so the hold refresh holds it until a receipt check (the mail watcher, or `pnpm mail:scan --receipt <run>`) finds the site's receipt, and it is never submitted again automatically.
A click that shows nothing recognized stays `SubmissionUnconfirmed` and is held the same way.
The answers are read back before the click as on every submit, and a stepped run walks again and holds if an approved answer changed; a stepped policy submit also gets the claimed-site check (Track 38).
CAPTCHAs and bot checks (reCAPTCHA, hCaptcha, Turnstile, Cloudflare and DataDome pages) are never solved: a visible one before or after the click holds the run, and its notice says it is the only step left for the candidate.
Candidate-fact rules are unchanged: voice drafts and AI-suggested drafts still hold a run, and nothing is invented.
A submit the candidate approves on these sites is unchanged: a recognized confirmation marks it submitted.
The fixtures are in-memory pages and the modelled stepped fixtures; no Oracle, Paylocity, Rippling, or Phenom confirmation page has been captured, so a live run's receipt is the proof.
Regressions: the Track 80 cases in `pnpm test:browser-worker:local` (`stepped-form-run.integration.test.ts`), `pnpm test:domain`, the final-submit cases in `pnpm test:browser-worker:fast`, and scenario (i) of `pnpm test:browser:stepped-forms`.
