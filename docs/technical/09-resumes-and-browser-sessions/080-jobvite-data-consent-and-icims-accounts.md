**Jobvite data consent and iCIMS accounts.**
With the standing data-consent setting on (`INTERNSHIP_AGENT_ACCEPT_DATA_CONSENT=1`), Jobvite's Data Consent step is taken on any run, at start, continue, and submit (`passJobviteConsent`, `packages/browser/src/ats/jobvite-consent.ts`; `acceptJobviteConsentStep`, `packages/agent/src/standing-consent.ts`): the "Location of Residence and Language" option naming the profile country is chosen (`chooseResidenceOption`: any spelling of the United States for a US profile, never a "Non-US" option, English kept when several match), the step's one Accept control is clicked, and the timeline logs "Accepted data consent: '<the policy text shown>' (Jobvite Data Consent at <host>; residence "<option>")"; then the form behind it is read and filled as usual.
No saved country, no single matching option (nothing is clicked), a CAPTCHA, or a step still shown after Accept stops the run with a plain reason; the setting off keeps the stop above. iCIMS accounts are opt-in (`INTERNSHIP_AGENT_ICIMS_ACCOUNTS=1` with `INTERNSHIP_AGENT_AUTO_APPLY=1`, manual checklist mode off, and an account email, `INTERNSHIP_AGENT_ACCOUNT_EMAIL` else `INTERNSHIP_AGENT_WORKDAY_ACCOUNT_EMAIL`, equal to the profile email; `icimsAccountSettings`) and apply to automatic runs only.
At iCIMS's email-first step (`ensureIcimsAccount`, `packages/agent/src/icims-account-service.ts`; screens read by `packages/browser/src/ats/icims-account.ts` in iCIMS's same-host content frame (`icims_content_iframe`, `?in_iframe=1`) when the career site draws one, else in the page; CAPTCHA detection covers both) the agent enters the account email, ticks the step's data-consent box only under the data-consent setting (logged "Accepted data consent: '<text>' (iCIMS email step at <host>)") and an attestation box, such as a privacy-notice acknowledgment ("I acknowledge I have reviewed and understand the … Applicant Privacy Notice"), only under the attestation setting (`INTERNSHIP_AGENT_SIGN_ATTESTATIONS=1`; logged "Accepted attestation: '<text>' (iCIMS email step at <host>)"); the statement is read from the box's own label, else from the box's own block (its highest ancestor in the form holding no other control, such as iCIMS's `#gdpr_container`, at most 600 characters) with screen-reader-only descriptions (the box's `aria-describedby` text and `.sr-only`, `.visually-hidden`, `.screen-reader-text` elements) removed, since they are not the statement the candidate sees, else from the text before the box's row; a marketing box stays unchecked; a required box needing a setting that is off holds as `consent-setting` (a data consent) or `attestation-setting` (an attestation), each released once its setting is on, and any other required box (a bare "I accept" beside empty legal text included) as `consent-unknown`, and then either creates the profile (a random 16-character password per host saved first in the owner-only `icims-accounts.json`, `INTERNSHIP_AGENT_ICIMS_ACCOUNTS_FILE`, default `~/.config/internship-agent/<profile>/icims-accounts.json`, required when `INTERNSHIP_AGENT_DATA_DIR` is set, marked `pending` just before the submit; the saved legal first and last name, the email, and the phone when saved; any other required field holds as `profile` before anything is typed) or signs in with the saved password.
An existing account with no saved password holds ("an iCIMS account exists for your email at <host> but no password is saved"); a refused or wrong saved password, a lockout, "already exists" after a create, and no answer to a create hold and are never retried; a CAPTCHA (hCaptcha included) holds and is never solved; an emailed verification link is read from the read-only Gmail mailbox (`findIcimsVerificationLink`: https, the tenant host or another `*.icims.com` host, an activation path) and opened in a new tab, then the agent signs in; an emailed code holds.
Every hold (`IcimsAccountHeld`) is stored with the host's entry, reported by the pipeline under `waiting` (reason `icims-account`, site key `icims:<host>`), logged "Automatic browser run waiting: …", saves `icims-account` evidence for the job, and keeps later passes from opening a browser until it allows (verification holds retry after 1 hour, doubling up to 24 hours).
The agent does not walk iCIMS's multi-page application yet: once the application shows, the run stops ("… but it does not fill iCIMS's multi-page application yet …"), saves `icims-application` evidence (screenshot and redacted text, for a follow-up adapter), and saves no run; iCIMS and Jobvite stay recognized kinds the candidate approves.
`pnpm icims:account [--profile real|demo]` lists hosts and their state; `--site <host or URL>` prints that host's email and password; `--site <host> --clear-hold` clears its hold (the password stays) so the next pass retries it.
