**Several-page forms on sites without an adapter (Track 74).**
On 2026-11-27 four applications were held only by the several-page stop: two Paylocity applications ("Next Step", "Step 1 of 5") and two Phenom career sites, MITRE's and a second Phenom career site ("Next", `step=1` in the apply page's address).
On an unknown or approval-only kind, a page with a Next control and no submit control that also shows its step count ("Step 1 of 5" or "Step 2/4" in its text, or `step=<n>` in its address) is walked step by step instead of stopped (`readStepProgress`, `steppedWalkEligible`, `walkSteppedForm` in `packages/browser/src/stepped-form.ts`).
Oracle Cloud HCM Candidate Experience, iCIMS, Handshake, and Workday pages are never walked this way, and a page without a step count keeps today's several-page stop.
Each step rejects a cookie banner first (reject or necessary-only, never accept), is planned with the same planner and inputs as a one-page form, and is filled; fields already filled on an earlier step of the walk are not planned again.
The walk then clicks only the step's own Next: a control whose whole text is Next, Continue, Save and continue, Next step, or Proceed and has no submit word (`tagStepNextIn`, `ApplicationPage.clickStepNext`); it never clicks a submit control, never types into a password field, and never solves a CAPTCHA or bot check.
It ends on the step that shows a submit control and no Next, where nothing is clicked and the run waits for the candidate's approval.
It pauses on an open question, on a field the page would not take, and on a field the site marks invalid after Next (the pause carries the site's error text).
It stops the run, with a step-transition capture and no run saved, on a later step with no step count or with its last step count still showing Next, on a step showing both a submit control and Next, on a step that did not move on after Next, on a password field, on what looks like a confirmation after Next, and after twelve steps.
Its field ids are `step-<n>::<id>` with n the site's own step number (`steppedFieldId`, `parseSteppedFieldId`, `isSteppedRun` in `packages/domain/src/stepped-form.ts`), like Workday's `<step name>::<id>`.
`packages/agent/src/stepped-form-run.ts` saves the run (kind unknown or the suggested kind, never confirmed automatically), continues it by walking again from the step the site shows with the candidate's answers and the run's own earlier decisions, and submits it only with the candidate's recorded approval: it walks again to the last step, holds the run if an approved answer changed, a choice did not take, a challenge shows, or the page is not at its last step, and then clicks the form's own submit control with `submitAndConfirm`.
Such a run is always the candidate's to approve: `autoSubmitBlockers` adds "This application continues over several pages on a site the agent has no adapter for, so you approve each one." (`STEPPED_FORM_BLOCKER`) for any stepped run, and `submitSteppedRun` refuses a policy approval before opening anything.
`ATS_KINDS_NEEDING_APPROVAL` is unchanged, so `paylocity` stays approval-only.
Clicking Next sends that step's answers to the employer's site before the final approval, as Workday's Save and Continue does; nothing is submitted until the candidate approves the last step.
The live Phenom and Paylocity steps after step 1 were never captured, so the fixtures are modelled (`testing-harness/browser/fixtures/stepped-phenom-application.html`, `stepped-paylocity-application.html`), and a live retry of the held applications is the only proof.
The regressions are `pnpm test:browser:stepped-forms` and the stepped cases in `pnpm test:browser-worker:fast` (`stepped-form.test.ts`) and `pnpm test:browser-worker:local` (`stepped-form-run.integration.test.ts`).
A step without any form control (a Review page) waits the full 15 seconds of the stable-controls read before it is read.
