**Previous internships, outstanding offers, and degree spellings (2026-11-28, Track 87).**
Two facts join the catalog (`packages/domain/src/candidate-facts.ts`), each global, standard, reused automatically, and saved only from the candidate's own answer.
`prior_internships_count` (text, one or two digits) is how many internships the candidate completed before.
It answers "How many internships have you completed (before this summer)?" and "Number of prior internships" wordings, and "Have you completed at least one previous internship?", "Have you ever interned before?" and "Do you have any prior internship experience?" Yes/No wordings; a wording naming a company stays previous employment, and one naming a field, paid work, or co-ops is not read (`isPriorInternshipCountQuestion`, `priorInternshipThreshold`, `packages/domain/src/fact-options.ts`).
A count list takes the one option holding the saved number ("0", "None", "1-2", "3+", "More than 3"; `internshipCountOption`), a text box gets the number, and an at-least Yes/No takes Yes when the saved number reaches the stated threshold, else No; a text box asking it gets the word, never the number (`factOptionDecision`, `packages/browser/src/autofill-planner.ts`).
`has_outstanding_offers` (boolean) is whether the candidate has outstanding or competing offers or offer deadlines; it answers "Do you have any outstanding job offers or deadlines right now?", "Are you holding any outstanding offers?", "Do you currently have any offers from other firms or deadlines we should be aware of?" and "Are you currently considering other offers?" (`OUTSTANDING_OFFERS`, `packages/agent/src/question-mapper.ts`).
A question that also asks to name the offers or their deadlines ("If so, please list the companies") is answered only by a saved No, a follow-up that only asks to name them stays unmapped, and an offer the employer makes ("accept an offer", "return offer") or a deadline asked with timing or school is not read.
A degree list offering two spellings of the one general bachelor's answer (Greenhouse's "Bachelors" beside "Bachelor's Degree", which held five runs in pass 37) takes the first offered; general options that differ in another word ("Bachelor's Level Degree", a parenthetical) still ask, and a B.S. is never taken for a B.A. (`chooseDegreeOption`, `packages/domain/src/application-history.ts`).
A degree list naming no bachelor's takes its one undergraduate option when that option is plain "Undergraduate" ("Undergraduate Degree", "Undergraduate Student"); two undergraduate options ("Undergraduate", "Undergraduate (4-year)") still ask.
The internship and offers questions are answered only after the candidate's answers are saved with `pnpm profile:facts`; no value is in a tracked file.
