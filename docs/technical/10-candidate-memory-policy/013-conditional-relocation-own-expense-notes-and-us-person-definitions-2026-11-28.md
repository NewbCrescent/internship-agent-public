**Conditional relocation, own-expense notes, and U.S.-person definitions (2026-11-28, Track 83).**
Track 83 lets the saved relocation, own-expense, on-site, and export-control answers reach held questions the deterministic rules already mapped to them but did not answer; the saved answers already existed.
A relocation destination is read without a conditional or later tail ("Fixture City if you are not already local" is "Fixture City"; "any of the locations below following the end of the internship" is "any of the locations below"), and "do so", "for this position", "any of …", "all …", "wherever …", "one of …", "either …", and "state/cities where …" are not places, so the posting's one country decides (`relocationPlace`, `packages/agent/src/question-mapper.ts`).
Living nearby asked as a condition ("If you do not live in the Fixture Bay area, are you able to relocate?", "… if you are not based locally?") counts like the "or" form, and the relocation Yes answers it (`relocationQuestionParts`, `packages/domain/src/fact-options.ts`).
A temporary move during the program or for the internship ("temporarily relocate … during the program") is the internship relocation itself and is answered by the relocation Yes; a temporary move not tied to the program still pauses.
A move following, after, or at the end of the internship reads as a move after the internship and pauses, like a permanent move.
A note that the employer pays nothing for the move ("we do not offer relocation assistance", "a separate relocation stipend will not be provided", "does not provide relocation support or housing", "relocate on your own") makes the question the own-expense question (`willing_to_relocate_at_own_expense`).
The own-expense question now gets the same part check as the plain one, without the who-pays part its answer settles: an on-site part needs the saved on-site Yes, and stated dates, a permanent move or one after the internship, or living nearby without "or" or "if" pause.
A relocation question naming a U.S. place uses the saved U.S.-wide answer of the same key: an own-expense question reads only the saved U.S. own-expense Yes, never the plain one (`usWideRelocationFact`, `packages/agent/src/candidate-service.ts`).
A saved relocation Yes on a list without a plain Yes option takes the one option stating the willingness ("I am willing to temporarily relocate"), never a negated one, and two such options pause (`relocationOption`).
A Yes/No question about U.S.-person status "including" its categories ("US person status (including US citizens, U.S. permanent residents, …)") is read as a U.S.-person question (`usPersonYesNoOption`, `packages/domain/src/standard-answers.ts`).
An option that names "U.S. person" first and then defines it ("U.S. person. This status includes U.S. citizens, U.S. nationals, lawful permanent residents …") is the U.S.-person option when its definition names the saved category, with plural category words read as singular, and never when the definition names a foreign or non-U.S. status; two such options pause (`usPersonOption`, `packages/domain/src/export-control.ts`).
Availability for a full-time offer or role, or after or upon graduation ("If you were to receive a full-time offer, when would you be available to start?", "I am available to begin a potential full-time role in 2028"), is no longer read as the internship start date; these questions stay with the candidate.
Still paused on purpose: stated dates or lengths, permanent moves or moves after the internship, questions asking the candidate to explain housing plans, options that differ on whether the candidate lives within commuting distance, location preferences, preferred start dates, and full-time availability after graduation.
