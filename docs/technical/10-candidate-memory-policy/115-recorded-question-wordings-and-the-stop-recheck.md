Recorded question wordings (migration 0036, `candidate_question_aliases`; `packages/db/src/question-alias-repository.ts`) keep each exact wording a stop asked, recorded against the saved answer it matched.
A wording points at a candidate fact by its key and scope key, or at a question-memory answer by its question key, and also keeps its normalized key, the run's ATS kind, the field kind, and the options the field offered; it never stores an answer value.
A wording is recorded when the candidate saves an answer from a held run (`answerRunPause`, source `candidate_answer`), when the stop re-check matches a stop to an already-saved answer (source `saved_answer_match`), and when the AI review of a pause batch matches one (source `ai_review`, see 117).
Recording the same wording, target, and ATS kind again updates its last-seen time and count instead of adding a row.
An answer used for one run only (`--once`, or any never-save rule) records no wording and re-checks nothing; a sensitive or protected answer gets a wording only when the answer itself was saved, and the re-check settles a sensitive stop only from the saved self-identification or pronouns answer (Track 91, below).
Field resolution reads catalog facts and standard answers first, then question memory, then recorded wordings, and only for a question the mapper leaves unmapped.
A recorded wording never widens a saved answer's scope: a global fact applies everywhere, a country fact only on a posting in that one country, a company fact only for that company, and location- and channel-scoped facts, protected facts, coursework, and standard answers never travel through a wording.
A question that names the posting's company or meets any never-reuse rule is never answered through a wording.
A field a recorded wording answered logs `Used a recorded wording of your saved answer for "<label>".` on the timeline, never the value.
The stop re-check (`recheckRunPauses` and `recheckPausedRuns` in `packages/agent/src/browser-orchestrator.ts`, helpers in `packages/agent/src/pause-recheck.ts`) runs after every saved answer and on `pnpm questions:recheck`.
It looks at every open question on every run paused for the candidate's input, except safeguards, uploads, the attached-resume confirmation, the security-code step, Workday error reports, faults, pauses without a recorded field kind, and page failures (a question starting "The agent could not fill").
Each such stop is planned again on a one-field form rebuilt from the pause, with today's saved answers and the same planner a new run uses, so the planner's own rules (fact-option rules, scope, sensitivity) still hold it when they would.
A sensitive stop (recorded sensitive, or a demographic field today) is settled only through the planner's deterministic self-identification path: a `SELF_IDENTIFIED` resolution from the candidate's saved voluntary self-identification answers (`matchSelfIdentificationOption` and the Track 82 and 90 rules) that the planner chooses or checks from its own key (Track 91, 2026-11-28).
A stop an older run recorded as sensitive whose field is no longer a demographic one (a pronoun question, Track 82) is settled only from the saved pronouns answer through the question mapper, never through a recorded wording or question memory; any other such stop stays open (`sensitiveStopSettlement`, `packages/agent/src/pause-recheck.ts`).
A sensitive stop is never sent to the AI review, records no wording, and keeps no answer: the pause is marked answered with no answer, and the timeline says `Answer to "<label>" settled by your saved voluntary self-identification answer; it no longer waits for you.` (or `your saved pronouns answer`), never the value or its category.
On continue such a stop is planned again on the page from the saved answers, and it opens again when today's plan no longer settles it.
A single sensitive checkbox (Oracle's "I am Hispanic or Latino." box, a veteran classification box) is never settled by the re-check in either direction: the pause record does not keep whether the field was optional, so only one direction could settle there, and settling it would show the saved answer; the continue that reads the page settles it.
Only a fill, choice, or check that comes from a saved fact, a saved question-memory answer, or the candidate's profile (basis `profile`: contact details, links, school, degree, field of study, GPA, and education dates, as a new run's planner takes them; Track 57) counts; a wording the AI review recorded is left for continue, where its answer is marked as an AI review match (see 117).
A match marks the pause answered with the planned answer (a fact match also keeps the fact's key and scope), the run stays held, and continue fills it (`pnpm run:answer --continue`, or the next pipeline pass for a run the pipeline started once no question is left open; Track 78).
The timeline then says `Answer to "<label>" found in your saved answers (<kind>): <saved answer>; it no longer waits for you.`, naming a fact by its label and scope key or a question-memory answer by its id and saved question, never the value.
A profile match names the profile field instead (`your profile's LinkedIn link`), records no wording, and on continue the field keeps the planner's profile basis and reason when today's plan gives the same answer.
The match kinds are `saved_fact` (the question mapper's key), `standard_answer`, `question_memory_exact` (same wording), `question_memory_normalized` (same wording after normalization), `alias_exact`, `alias_normalized` (a recorded wording), `profile_field` (the profile), and `self_identification` (a sensitive stop the saved self-identification or pronouns answer settles; Track 91).
Before writing, the re-check reads the run again and writes nothing when it is no longer held or changed since it was planned.
`pnpm profile:answers` lists each saved answer with its recorded wordings (ATS kind, option count, source, times seen, last seen, alias id), then the saved facts with recorded wordings, protected values shown only as `saved`; `--revoke-alias <id or 8-character prefix>` revokes one wording.
`pnpm questions:recheck [--profile real|demo] [--apply]` prints, per held run, the questions saved answers would answer and their kinds, then the totals (open pauses, answered by kind, skipped, still open, and how many would reach the AI review), counts and labels only.
Its default dry run opens the database read-only and writes nothing; `--apply` marks the matches answered under the pipeline lock and is refused in manual checklist mode.
