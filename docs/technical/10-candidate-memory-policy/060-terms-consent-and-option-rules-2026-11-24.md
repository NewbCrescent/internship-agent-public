**Terms consent and option rules (2026-11-24).**
The data-consent setting also accepts a site's terms and conditions or terms of use accepted as a condition of applying (the standing group `terms-of-use`): forward phrasings ("I agree with the terms and conditions", "I accept the Terms of Use"), combined terms-and-privacy "Personal Data" boxes, and "<heading>: I agree" where the heading names terms, a privacy notice, or personal data; the terms and conditions of employment or of an offer are not site terms.
An optional terms box or list is left alone (unchecked), and the same fact and marketing vetoes apply first, so terms bundled with marketing, newsletters, job alerts, text messages, or a statement of fact are never accepted.
A statement that also asks about text messages never joins the terms group; the candidate's text-message rule decides it (it runs first in the planner).
Greenhouse's one-option checkbox question (a fieldset whose one box is labelled only "I agree and consent") is read as "<legend>: <label>".
Saved facts pick an offered option by deterministic rules (`packages/domain/src/fact-options.ts`, planner `factOptionDecision`): the salary rule (the posting's stated pay) agrees with an advertised range asked as a list ("If posted, are you comfortable with the advertised salary range?" maps to `salary_expectation_rule` on a list, never typed or suggested; a text box keeps the expectation); `willing_to_relocate_at_own_expense` picks the own-expense option of a combined "in or near the city?
If not, relocate at your own expense?" list, and "already located" only when the profile's "City, Region" equals the posting's one place exactly (start runs only; Workday runs pass no places), while a saved No pauses; the offer-relative "how soon could you start" buckets are chosen from today and `available_start_date` (the matching bucket with the latest start, so a far date picks "6+ weeks" and "Immediately" is never chosen before the date; no bucket or a tie pauses); background-check willingness questions ("Are you willing to undergo a background check?") map to `willing_background_check`, never "pass", consent, or authorization wording.
A react-select multi-select's chosen chip counts as the list keeping the choice, and an already-chosen chip is not chosen again.
U.S.-person and start-date rules (2026-11-25): an export-control question whose "Are you a U.S. person?" sentence follows a statement ("You must be a U.S. Person because … Are you a US Person?") maps to the derived `us_person` Yes/No; a list whose options name no export-control category picks, for a saved status naming a U.S.-person category, the one option saying the candidate is a U.S. person now ("I am currently a "U.S.
Person""), never a "will soon become" or "not … but eligible" option, and nothing for a saved "none" status (`usPersonOption`).
Start-date questions also read "earliest/soonest … start", "when can you (be able to) start or begin", "timeline for starting", and "When would you be available (if an offer was accepted)?"
(micron.wd1, Track 32), never availability "for an interview" or "to work …", a full-time or after-graduation start, or a yes/no question; offer-relative options that end "after offer acceptance" ("Within 1 month after offer acceptance") are read as the same buckets, and "After Graduation" names no window; the saved date is typed as a date input YYYY-MM-DD, into a text box in the format its label names (DD/MM/YYYY, YYYY-MM-DD, MM-DD-YYYY), else MM/DD/YYYY.
Workday's split `dateSectionMonth/Day/Year-input` boxes outside the history sections are a date field (`workday-date`) the adapter types month, day, and year from YYYY-MM-DD or MM/DD/YYYY; another format is a field the page would not take, which pauses.
F-1 OPT (Track 32): the derived `f1_optional_practical_training` answers only Yes/No "Are you currently on (in a period of) OPT?" and "Are you eligible for the (24-month STEM) OPT extension?" questions (the acronym only in capitals, never "opt in"/"opt-out"): No from a saved Yes to U.S. citizenship or a saved citizen/national or permanent-resident export-control status (OPT exists only for F-1 students), otherwise the candidate answers; it is never Yes and never stored, and any other OPT question (a date, a future need, combined with citizenship) pauses.
