**Known answers on Lever and Greenhouse forms (2026-11-26, Track 43).**
"Do you have the unrestricted right to work in the United States?" (also "unrestricted work authorization") maps to the derived `unrestricted_us_work_authorization`, never stored: Yes from a saved U.S. citizenship Yes or a saved citizen/national or permanent-resident export-control status, No from a saved No to U.S. work authorization, and otherwise, or when those disagree, the candidate answers; a question that also names sponsorship, citizenship, government, or export-control wording, or another country, does not map.
A current-student question ("Are you a current student enrolled in a college/university program or have graduated within the past 12 months?") maps to `enrolled_full_time` as a broader question (`broader` on the mapping): only a saved Yes answers it, a saved No pauses, and an answer given to it is used for that run only; a question naming a degree level, a major, a country, part-time study, or timing does not map.
A saved graduation date picks a term option (`graduationTermOption`, `packages/domain/src/fact-options.ts`): a month ("December 2027", "Dec 2027"), a season (Spring April–June, Summer July–August, Fall or Autumn September–December; Winter is never read), and an open end ("December 2028 or later", "Spring 2026 or earlier"); the one option holding the date's month is chosen, and none, several, or fewer than two readable options pause.
The graduation answer is the one the run's profile uses, so a run on the transcript profile picks the term of that profile's date.
A sentence-style GPA label ("Please specify the overall average grade (GPA) for your last/current degree:", "Please enter your GPA") takes the profile GPA like a GPA label, and when the GPA falls in none of a list's ranges because of their precision ("3.0 - 3.29", "3.3 - 3.69"), it is read truncated to each option's decimals, so it never moves up a range.
"Please select your field of study:" takes the profile's field of study, and a list of broad categories takes the one option naming it as a whole phrase (`fieldOfStudyCategoryOption`, `packages/domain/src/application-history.ts`), never an option that negates it ("Engineering (Non-Computer Science)") or "Other"; none or several pause.
"Where are you currently located?" asked as a list of countries ("United States of America", "Other (International)") takes the one option naming the country of the profile's address; another list, or no saved country, pauses.
Ranking and preference questions (roles, tech-stack interest, programming languages), availability commitments, project links, and "I understand that next steps will be sent via email and I have marked the sender address above as safe" (it states an action of the candidate's that the attestation setting cannot know) stay with the candidate.
