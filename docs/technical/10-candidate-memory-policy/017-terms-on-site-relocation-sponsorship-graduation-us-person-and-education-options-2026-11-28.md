**Internship terms, on-site relocation, sponsorship, graduation, U.S.-person and education options (2026-11-28, Track 89).**
Term and season questions ("Which term(s) are you applying for?", "Are you interested in Spring Co-Op, Summer Internship, or Both?", "Please confirm the season you are applying for.") take the one offered summer term the saved availability window falls in (`internshipTermAnswer`, `packages/domain/src/term-availability.ts`).
The window is the candidate's saved `available_start_date` and `available_end_date`, both saved by the candidate for automatic reuse (`reusableAvailabilityWindow`, `packages/agent/src/candidate-service.ts`), and it must lie inside May–September of one year.
A term option qualifies only when summer is its only season, every year it names is the window's year, and every span it states lies inside the window: a month span by months ("Summer (May - August)"), a day span by days ("June 1st to August 31st"); "Both", "Either", co-op options, and any other date words never qualify.
A Yes/No summer availability confirmation ("I confirm my availability for a Summer 2027 (May/June starts) internship", "Are you available to commit to a Summer internship from May - August 2027?") takes Yes only when the window covers every date it states and it asks nothing more (relocation, on-site work, hours, weeks or months, full-time work, or graduation).
A text box asking for the dates of internship availability gets the window as "MM/DD/YYYY - MM/DD/YYYY".
A span or date outside the window ("(May - September 2027)", "(May 10th - August 20th, 2027)"), another season or year, or a window that is not saved for automatic reuse answers nothing, so the field pauses.
A start-date or start-month list where several offered dates fall inside the window is not answered (exactly one option must match).
A term question's answer may be remembered for the same wording: "Which term are you interested in?" is not a why-interest question (`questionSaveBlocker`, `packages/domain/src/question-answer-memory.ts`).
A relocation question's covered summer term or span ("(June-August)", "(Summer 2027)") is read away before its other parts are checked (`withoutCoveredTerm`), and the role's own location ("This role is located in <city> …") is not read as asking where the candidate lives (`relocationQuestionParts`, `packages/domain/src/fact-options.ts`).
An own-expense option "Yes, I will arrange my own relocation to <city> …" is the own-expense option (`relocationOption`).
For a saved on-site Yes on a posting whose one stated country is the United States, with a saved U.S.-wide relocation Yes for automatic reuse (`usRelocationSettles`, `packages/agent/src/saved-answers.ts`), an on-site list takes its one "Yes, I will relocate …" option (`onSiteRelocationOption`, `packages/domain/src/on-site-relocation.ts`); an option that claims living there, names relocation help, remote work, a qualification, or another date never qualifies, and several relocating options pause.
"Are you excited to work in-office …?" maps to the saved on-site answer.
A question naming a list of offices or locations where several apply, and a commuting-distance question, stay paused.
"… require, or will you in the future require, employment-based immigration sponsorship?" maps to sponsorship scoped to the posting's country; a posting with no single stated country (no location, only "Remote", or both the United States and the United Kingdom) keeps it paused.
"Are you on EAD, OPT or CPT? Or need a H-1B visas transfer?" maps to the F-1 OPT question, which a saved U.S. citizenship answers No; a negated wording stays paused.
A visa-kind list's "Not applicable" or "N/A" is the no-sponsorship option for a saved No.
More graduation wordings map to the graduation date ("When do you graduate?", "What year are you expected to graduate?", "the year you anticipate graduating", "When do you expect to complete your current degree program?"), and a list of bare years ("2026" … "2030") takes the year holding the saved date.
An earliest full-time start question is not settled by the saved answers and stays paused.
A U.S.-person list asked as "Please select appropriate response:", an export-regulations "which of the following applies to you" list, and a visa or immigration status list map to the saved export-control status, which picks only the one option naming its category; a visa-status text box stays paused.
"Are you one of the following: a U.S. citizen, a U.S. national, …?" is a U.S.-person Yes/No, and "U.S. person. This ITAR/EAR status includes …" is a defined U.S.-person option.
School text boxes and lists read more labels ("Current school or university:", "What college/university do you attend?", "What is the name of your university?", "Which university are you currently attending or did you last attend?").
A list that asks for "Other" when the school is not listed takes its one "Other" option only when the list has fewer than 500 options and no other option shares a distinctive word with the profile's school.
"What is your field of study/degree program?" takes the profile's field of study, and "What degree are you currently pursuing or have recently completed?" is answered like a degree list.
GPA labels also read "Please indicate your overall GPA.", "What is your overall college/university GPA?", "For your most recent degree, what is/was your GPA (normalized to a 4.0 scale)?", and "If you are … within the past 24 months, what is your current or final GPA?".
A GPA scale list ("Please select the corresponding GPA scale:") takes its one 4.0-scale option for a profile GPA of at most 4.0.
A SAT list stating both the 1600 and the 2400 scale is read on the 1600 scale only (the ACT on its 36 scale).
Clearance history (ever denied, revoked, expired, most recent, or a prescreen questionnaire), the highest level of education completed, class standing, an acknowledgment-only option, and clipped labels stay paused: no saved answer states them.
