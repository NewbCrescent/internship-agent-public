**Standing consent and attestations.**
Two explicit candidate settings (2026-11-20), read by `standingConsentSettings` (`packages/domain/src/standing-consent.ts`) and both off in manual checklist mode: `INTERNSHIP_AGENT_ACCEPT_DATA_CONSENT=1` accepts data-processing, data-retention, and privacy-policy consent that is a condition of applying; `INTERNSHIP_AGENT_SIGN_ATTESTATIONS=1` signs accuracy certifications ("I certify that the information … is true", "false statements may result in …"), electronic signatures, acknowledgments (at-will employment, a privacy or applicant notice read, a temporary internship, an equal-opportunity statement), and background-check, consumer-report, drug-test, reference-check, employment-verification, and arbitration authorizations.
A notice that the process will require a background check, employment or education verification, or a drug screen ("you will be required … to submit to a criminal background check …", answered "Acknowledged" on littelfuse.wd1, 2026-11-25) counts as such an authorization when it asks no question.
The classifier (`classifyStandingStatement`, `standingStatementDecision`) is a deterministic whitelist of phrasings per group.
Statements of fact about the candidate veto acceptance first and fall through to the usual rules (a saved fact or question-memory answer fills them, else they pause): citizenship, U.S. person, export control and ITAR, government or DoD eligibility and clearance, FDA/OIG debarment and sanctions, conflicts of interest, criminal-history assertions (an authorization that only mentions criminal history is not one), prior employment, non-compete and restrictive covenants, relatives, work authorization and sponsorship, age (including "18 or older"), enrolment and degree, demographic self-identification (a demographic veto for self-identification phrasing: "I identify as…", "I am a veteran/female/…", "my gender/race…", "self-identify"), and, as a separate first-person-fact veto, any other "I am …" assertion ("I am able to work onsite…") except certification verbs ("the information I am providing/submitting…"), so an ordinary accuracy certification is signed; `factGroupsOf` names every fact group a statement matches.
Marketing, newsletter, job-alert, talent-community, and text-message opt-ins are never accepted by these settings (texts follow the text-message rule below): an optional one stays unchecked and a required one pauses ("marketing opt-in: needs your OK").
A statement spanning both kinds needs both settings, and a label recognition clipped ("…") is never accepted.
`standingFieldDecision` (`packages/browser/src/standing-fields.ts`) runs in `planAutofill` before the sensitive-role rule (a sensitive list or radio set never reaches it; a single checkbox may): a single checkbox is checked, a consent list or radio set takes its one affirmative option ("Yes", "I agree"), a text box whose label names an electronic signature takes the saved legal first and last name (else the full name; with neither it pauses), and a "Date" box beside a signature takes today's local date in the label's format (MM/DD/YYYY, DD/MM/YYYY, YYYY-MM-DD, MM-DD-YYYY; a date input takes YYYY-MM-DD; else MM/DD/YYYY).
A "Have you read the … Privacy Policy?" list or radio set (littelfuse.wd1, Track 32) is decided on the question and its one affirmative option together (`privacyReadStatement`, "<question> — <option>"): an option that also consents ("Yes, I have read and consent to the Global Privacy Policy") is privacy-policy consent under the data-consent setting and is logged with that combined exact text; a bare "Yes" is a privacy-notice acknowledgment under the attestation setting; without the setting it falls to the usual rules and pauses.
A list or radio set with only one option (Track 67) is decided on its label and that option together (`singleOptionStatement`, "<label>: <option>", or "<label> — <option>" after end punctuation): Ashby's "Arbitration Acknowledgment:" whose only option is "I Acknowledge/Accept this Statement" is an arbitration authorization under the attestation setting, "GDPR Acknowledgement" with that option is privacy-policy consent under the data-consent setting, a privacy-notice list whose only option is "Yes" or "I have read the Privacy Notice." is a privacy-notice acknowledgment, each logged with that combined text, while a fact the option asserts ("Confirming my response to the US Persons question is correct.") or an unknown heading ("Candidate AI Usage Attestation: I agree") is never accepted.
A statement that the candidate took an action the agent cannot know ("I have marked the sender address above as safe") is a statement of fact and stays with the candidate.
Without the attestation setting a signature box always pauses ("electronic signature: needs your OK"); without the data-consent setting a data-retention box pauses as before (`CONSENT_QUESTION`).
Each decision records basis `standing_consent` (setting, group, the label as read), and each applied one is logged on the timeline with the exact text (`logStandingAcceptances`, `packages/agent/src/standing-consent.ts`): "Accepted data consent: '<text>'", "Accepted attestation: '<text>'", "Accepted data consent and attestation: '<text>'", "Signed attestation: '<label>' — signed as <legal name>", "Dated the signature: '<label>' as <date>".
The legal name stays in local SQLite (events and run decisions) and is never written to console output, errors, reports, or notices.
These decisions do not block the auto-submit policy (the candidate's opt-in, 2026-11-20: `autoSubmitBlockers` does not count them); its approval event adds "consent and attestation statements were accepted under your standing settings".
With either setting on, a marketing opt-in asked as a Yes/No list or radio set takes its one "No" (declined, even when the label was clipped); a statement about the application itself ("text messages about your application status") is not declined; texts follow the text-message rule below.
A modal dialog over the form is agreed to only under the same classifier and settings (Track 40, `09-resumes-and-browser-sessions/137-standing-consent-dialogs.md`).
