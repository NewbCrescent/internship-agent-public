**ACT not taken, full-time availability, county, clearance lists, and whole-address labels (2026-11-28, Track 83, round 2).**
Three facts join the catalog (`packages/domain/src/candidate-facts.ts`), each global, standard, reused automatically, and saved only from the candidate's own answer.
`act_taken` (boolean) says whether the candidate took the ACT; with no saved `act_score`, a saved No answers an ACT score question by choosing the list's one "did not take" option ("Did not take/Do not recall", "I don't have ACT score", "N/A"), and a score is never derived from it.
A saved `act_score` always wins over `act_taken`, and a saved Yes to taking the ACT answers nothing by itself (`resolveCandidateQuestion`, `packages/agent/src/candidate-service.ts`).
An ACT score text box, or a list without exactly one "did not take" option, pauses: the saved No is never typed or chosen as "No" (`factOptionDecision`, `packages/browser/src/autofill-planner.ts`).
`available_full_time` (boolean) is the candidate's availability to work full-time (40 hours a week) during the saved internship window.
It answers full-time work questions ("Are you able to commit to a full-time internship (40 hours per week) from <date> - <date>?", "Are you available to work full time?"), never a full-time role or offer, a start after the internship or graduation, a fall or semester start, part-time work, or a student status, which keeps `enrolled_full_time` (`FULL_TIME_WORK`, `packages/agent/src/question-mapper.ts`).
A question stating two full dates is answered only when both fall inside the saved `available_start_date`..`available_end_date`, a stated length ("12-week", "three months") only when it fits in that window, and other date words hold; a saved No answers only a question with no dates (`availabilityWindowHold`, `packages/domain/src/fact-options.ts`).
`county` (text) is the county of the current address; it answers "County", "County of residence", and "What county do you live in?" labels, never "County of birth".
A county list matches the saved county by name, without "County" or "Parish", and an option with a ", XX" state suffix matches only the profile's state (`countyOption`); a text box gets the saved text.
A saved No to holding a clearance picks the one plain none option ("None of the Above", "None/Not Required") of a list naming clearance levels; options saying never held, expired, inactive, or prefer not to disclose never count (`clearanceNoneOption`, `packages/domain/src/standard-answers.ts`).
A "What type of clearance do you currently have?" list maps to holding a clearance, while the same wording as a text box, and "current or most recent" or former clearance wordings, stay with the candidate (`clearanceQuestion`, `packages/agent/src/standard-question-mapper.ts`).
A "(Requires U.S. Citizenship)" note after a clearance eligibility question is a requirement note, so the question maps to clearance eligibility instead of holding as a combined citizenship question.
An address asked whole ("Full address", "Address (City, State, Zip)", or a text area labelled "Address" or "Home Address") gets the profile address on one line only when street, city, state and ZIP are all saved; a bare "Mailing / Home / Permanent / Current / Residential Address" text box gets the street line only when the form also asks the city or ZIP (`addressDecision`).
The stop re-check plans one field alone, so a bare "Mailing Address" stop stays paused there; "(Home|Mailing|Permanent) Street Address" keeps the existing contact rule, and type-ahead address boxes are untouched.
Still held: clearances ever held, expired, inactive within two years, denied, suspended or revoked, willingness to complete a security prescreen, Security+ certification, and proof of U.S. citizenship, which no saved answer settles.
