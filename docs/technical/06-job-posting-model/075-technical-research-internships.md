**Technical research internships.**
At the candidate's request (2026-11-26, "I am fine with research summer"), `judgeRole` also counts a research title for the target term when the research is technical: AI/ML, computer science, robotics and autonomy, systems, security, HCI as computer science, and quantitative research.
A title that names research (`research`, `researcher`) passes unless it also names non-technical research (market, consumer, user, design, equity, investment, economic, social, policy, clinical, medical, health, biology, wet lab, and the like) without a computing word (computational, robotics, autonomy, systems, quantitative, HCI, security, and the like), which skips with "Role: \"<word>\" research is not technical research."; UX, design, policy, clinical, marketing, and trading titles skip as before.
A title named for graduate students skips with "Role: \"<word>\" in the title names a graduate-level role." (PhD, doctoral, doctorate, Master's, MBA, research scientist), unless it also names a bachelor's level (BS, BA, bachelor's, undergraduate), so "Research Intern (BS/MS/PhD)" counts and "PhD Research Intern" does not; matching's `titleNamesGraduateStudy` reads a title the same way, while graduate-level requirements still fail matching and never reach auto-submit.
The candidate added "as long as it is payed": `judgePay` rules a research posting out with "Pay: research internship is unpaid or for credit." when it states it is unpaid, for academic or course credit only or in lieu of pay, volunteer, uncompensated, without pay, or that a stipend is not provided, and every other internship with the same wording keeps "Pay: the posting says the internship is unpaid."; a research posting silent on pay counts, since industry research internships are usually paid without saying so.
The pipeline judges the target on every pass, so stored postings this widens are picked up by the next pass without a data change or command; matching verdicts are stored, so a posting matching already ruled NOT_QUALIFIED for a "BS/MS"-style title keeps that verdict until it is re-analyzed, which `pnpm matches:reevaluate` does for stored postings.
Regressions: `testing-harness/domain/application-target.test.ts` (`pnpm test:domain`), and `testing-harness/matching/eligibility-fit.test.ts` and `review-pursuit.test.ts` (`pnpm test:matching:fast`).
