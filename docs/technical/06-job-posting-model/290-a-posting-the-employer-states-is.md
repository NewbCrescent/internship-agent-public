A posting the employer states is for one school's students fails for a candidate at another school (`school-restriction.ts`): a title led by the school ("MIT- Software Engineer Intern"), "<School> students only" or "(<School> students)" in the title, or text saying the role is open only to students at a school.
Only these shapes count, so a location ("Columbia, MD"), a company ("Duke Energy"), or a passing mention never does.
The profile school is compared by the same school patterns; with no school recorded the finding pauses.
