The candidate decided on 2026-11-26 that application education history lists only the university by default, and that the high school is entered only where a form asks for it.
Real Workday runs at seven companies had paused on the high-school row, because the Degree list offered no high-school option, Field of Study had nothing that fit, or the school list lacked the school.
`educationRowsForSection`, `asksForHighSchool`, and `asksForAllSchools` (`packages/domain/src/application-history.ts`) hold the rule: a label naming high school, secondary school, or GED asks for the high school, a label asking for every (all, each) school attended asks for every school, and every other education section gets the college rows only.
The bare word "diploma" does not ask for the high school, since Workday degree lists label themselves "Degree / Diploma"; "High school diploma" still does.
Workday reads its Education section's own label (`aria-labelledby`, else its first heading), so My Experience gets the college row, and a high-school row already on the page is deleted and recorded as a `remove` decision like any row that is not the candidate's.
The planner's school-question rule (`schoolQuestionDecision` in `packages/browser/src/autofill-planner.ts`) runs before the contact and college rules: a question naming high school gets the saved high school's name, start or graduation year, city, state, ZIP code, or "City, State", typed, searched, or the one fitting option; anything not saved or not offered pauses when required and skips when optional.
A high-school question never takes the college's graduation date: a high-school date asks the candidate, since only the high school's years are saved.
A question asking for every school attended gets one line per school, the college first, then the high school, only when both are saved; a single-line box joins them with "; ".
`PlannerInputs.schools` carries the rows and the high school's place, wired in `plannerInputsFor` (`packages/agent/src/browser-orchestrator.ts`, where a college end year comes only from the run's graduation answer) and `planWorkdayStep` (`packages/agent/src/workday-run.ts`).
Migration 0035 adds `high_school_city`, `high_school_region`, `high_school_postal_code`, and `high_school_source` to `candidates`; they are not on the profile page, and a profile save keeps them.
`pnpm profile:facts --set-file` accepts a `highSchool` file that fills only the saved high school's missing details with a provenance note, keeps a saved value that differs, refuses a file naming another school, and prints detail names, never values.
Its `--dry-run` writes nothing, but opening the database still applies pending migrations.
