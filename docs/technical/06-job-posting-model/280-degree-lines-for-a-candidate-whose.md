Degree lines for a candidate whose profile degree is a bachelor's are read word by word (`degree-reading.ts`) before the older token-overlap fallback.
A line passes only when every word is understood: a run of degree levels that includes a bachelor's ("BS, MS, or PhD", "Bachelor's or Advanced degree", "Bachelor's degree or higher"), a field list that names the profile field of study or asks for a STEM, technical, technology, quantitative, or scientific field, and ordinary enrollment wording.
"Information Technology" is its own field, a family word after "related" counts only when no other field is listed ("EE, ME, or a related technical field" stays a judgment), and graduation-window words are understood only when the posting's graduation constraint checks them separately.
Anything else (full-time, class year, GPA, credit hours, a country, "(obtained)", a high-school diploma, enrollment after the internship, "applicable major") leaves the line for review.
The graduate-only exclusion uses the same level runs, so "BS, MS, or PhD" is no longer read as graduate-only.
The exclusion also reads a deterministic line left PREFERRED only for lack of any cue (confidence 0.4) when it states current graduate enrollment ("Current students pursuing a master's degree in …") and has no preference wording; a Preferred heading or "preferred" keeps it soft.
"Full-time" in a degree line is understood only when the candidate's `enrolled_full_time` answer is yes; while it is unrecorded, a line that would otherwise pass says to answer "Are you a full-time student?" in candidate memory, and a no leaves the line for review rather than failing it.
A B.S. and a B.A. are different degrees: a line that names only one kind ("B.S.", "Bachelor of Science", "BS/MS") and not "bachelor's" or "undergraduate" in general pauses for a candidate with the other kind (`bachelorKindMismatch`), ahead of both the word-level reader and the token-overlap fallback.
