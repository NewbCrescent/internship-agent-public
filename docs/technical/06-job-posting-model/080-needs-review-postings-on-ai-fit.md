**Needs-review postings on AI fit.**
At the candidate's request (2026-11-24), `advancePipeline` also prepares an ANALYZING posting whose deterministic verdict is NEEDS_REVIEW when `reviewPursuit` (packages/matching `review-pursuit.ts`) allows it and a target is set: the match is eligible with no failed finding; every open finding is a skill or experience neither evidence nor the resume names, degree wording the reader cannot compare, or a B.S./B.A. mismatch; no open finding, requirement (required or preferred), or title names graduate-only study; no open requirement is a statement of fact (clearance, citizenship, U.S.-person or export-control status, work authorization, sponsorship); the job has no package and no browser run; and the combined target decision (term, role, US location, source, and `judgeFit` at the auto-submit bar, 50 by default, or 80 when auto-submit is off) is `apply`, which needs a current AI fit at or above the bar.
The minimum fit, discovery skips, label holds, the company cap, and the graduation-profile rule apply as for qualified postings; NOT_QUALIFIED is never pursued, and a posting without a current AI fit (none yet, stale, or a recent failure) stays in ANALYZING until the normal fit step gives it one.
The PREPARING transition is logged as "Pursued on AI fit N despite unverified requirements: …" (requirement texts); only ANALYZING postings move, so terminal and in-flight statuses are never regressed.
Automatic runs of such a posting start only while its decision is still `apply`; auto-submit uses the unchanged `autoSubmitBlockers` and names the unverified requirements in its approval basis.
The pass log, `pipeline-status.json` (`pursuedOnFit`), and the Discord notice's held and sent items list them.
Postings already waiting in ANALYZING are picked up by the next normal pass; no data change or command is needed.
