Three states stay distinct, because Milestone 4 eligibility depends on the difference:

- a `STATED` value the posting asserts at a recorded span;
- an `INFERRED` reading, which can never be a hard requirement — enforced by the domain schema and by a `CHECK` constraint on `job_requirements`;
- an absent value, meaning the posting is silent. Silence is never converted into a decision.
Separately, stated soft-trait skill and non-tenure experience requirements naming no concrete tool, method, credential, quantity, or tenure span pass by policy as non-discriminating rather than pausing verdicts; mixed, technical, degree, and tenure ("2+ years", "several years") wording stays on the evidence path.
