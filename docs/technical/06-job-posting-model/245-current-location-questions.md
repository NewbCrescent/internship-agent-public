A question asking for the candidate's own current location ("Location", "Current location", "Location (City)", "Where are you located?", "Where are you currently based?") is answered from the profile's city and state as "City, Region", with the profile as its basis; when the profile lacks a city or state, the candidate's saved answer to that exact wording (question memory) is used instead, with that answer as its basis.
A type-ahead takes that value typed in and picks the one suggestion that matches it (`matchSuggestion`); with two matching suggestions or none, the field is left unfilled and the run pauses on it.
Neither a posting's location nor the candidate's relocation answer ever answers a current-location question.
A question about where the candidate would work or be during the program, an office preference ("Which of the following office locations would be your first preference?"), or the location applied for is not the current location and pauses for the candidate (Track 71).
