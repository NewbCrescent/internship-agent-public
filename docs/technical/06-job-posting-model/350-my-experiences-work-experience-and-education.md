My Experience's Work Experience and Education sections are filled by `packages/browser/src/ats/workday-history.ts` from `planApplicationHistory` (packages/domain).
Migration 0021 adds the candidate's high school (`high_school_name`, start and end year) to the profile and `in_work_history` to resume entries (off by default, experience entries only, toggled on the Resume page with Add to / Remove from work history, independent of printing).
Work rows are the approved experience entries in work history: company, role as job title, location, start and end month (or I currently work here), and the entry's bullets verbatim as the role description.
Education rows are the college (profile school, degree, field of study, GPA, education start year, and the end year of the graduation answer the application uses) and then the high school.
Sections are found by Workday's `workExperienceSection` / `educationSection` markers or their headings, rows by `role="group"` panels, and fields by their labels.
Sections are found first as Workday's `role="group"` labelled (`aria-labelledby`) "Work Experience" or "Education", then by the markers, then by their headings; an Add is Workday's `add-button` or a button reading Add or Add Another, and one element is never taken for both sections (Track 36, employer-e.wd5 2026-11-25, cause unconfirmed).
Split month/year dates are typed into `dateSectionMonth-input` / `dateSectionYear-input`; Workday draws a `dateSection…-display` over each input that takes no click, so each section input is focused and typed into, never clicked, and other history inputs fall back to focusing when a click is not taken within 5 s. A degree option must be the one option of the same bachelor's kind as the profile degree, else the one general "Bachelor's" ("Bachelor's Degree", "Bachelor's Level Degree"); a B.A. never becomes a B.S., and two options that fit equally make the row ask.
Schools and fields of study are searched and matched by their words ("UC Berkeley" is "University of California, Berkeley"); a field of study also takes the one option whose words differ only by a plural ending ("Computer sciences" for "Computer Science", `matchFieldOfStudyOption`), and two such options make the row ask.
A row already showing the same company or school is reused, blank rows are used before Add, and a row that is not the candidate's is deleted and recorded as a `remove` decision.
Anything unmatched (a degree or school the site does not offer, a missing required date) pauses with the options Workday offered; the candidate's answer is used on the next continue.
Fields inside the history sections are left out of the ordinary step reading.
Before each Add, Delete, or dropdown click in a history row the adapter closes any list still open (Workday's multi-select Field of Study list stays open after a pick and covers the controls below it), and a click not taken within 5 s falls back to focusing the control and pressing Enter (`clickWorkdayControl` in `workday-lists.ts`, also used for every Workday dropdown); nothing is force-clicked.
An Add that shows no new row within 8 s is clicked once more after closing lists; a second miss asks the candidate to add the row.
Before each field of a history row the adapter closes any open list and re-tags the sections, since Workday can re-draw a row and drop the tags (zoll.wd5, 2026-11-25); a control that still does not respond within 5 s is retried once and then becomes that row's question, never a crash, and the high-school row is handled the same way.
Education dates are found by their labels or by Workday's `formField-firstYearAttended` / `formField-lastYearAttended` containers; a row with no end-date field asks nothing (Workday's own check decides), and a date field neither read pauses with its label.
A required Field of Study on a row with nothing saved for it (a high school) pauses as that row's question before the step is saved, and the answer is used on the next continue.
A high-school row picks a high-school diploma option, then a plain high-school option, then a combined diploma-or-equivalent option; a GED or equivalency option alone is never picked.
