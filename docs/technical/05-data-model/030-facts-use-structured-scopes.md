Facts use structured scopes:

- `global`
- `country` (work authorization, sponsorship, and relocation willingness)
- `location`
- `application_channel` (only `handshake_internal`, and only for `graduation_date`)
- `company` (the posting's employer, normalized: lower case, punctuation dropped, trailing legal suffixes such as Inc or LLC removed; exact match, never fuzzy; only `previously_worked_here` and `relatives_at_company`, whose Yes must be company-scoped). A previous-employment question is this employer's when it says "here" or "this company", names the posting's company, or, on a Workday application, names the site's tenant as consecutive whole words (`mentionsSiteTenant`: micron for micron.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com, "Highmark Health" for highmarkhealth), so "employed by any <tenant> company" or "employed by <Tenant Words> … or its subsidiaries" is answered with the posting company's scope; "Did you work here before?" counts. It also counts when it names the posting's company by its leading word or words alone (`mentionsCompanyLead`: "Have you worked for Constellation before?" for Example Brands), only capitalized, right after "for", "at", "by", "with", "within", or "of", and never followed by another capitalized word ("Constellation Energy" is another company). A family-of-companies question naming neither the posting's company nor the site's tenant (a parent group "or any of its subsidiaries", employer-f.wd1 for National Interstate) stays with the candidate. A saved No never answers a previous-employment question that names a company the candidate has a saved Yes for; that question asks the candidate.
